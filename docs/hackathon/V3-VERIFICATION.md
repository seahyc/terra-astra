# v3 implementation / v0.6 milestone

13 September 2026. Current authority: [EXECUTION-V3.md](EXECUTION-V3.md). Earlier plans, snapshots and v0.5 demo remain historical records.

## Delivered renderer scope

The existing Three point pipeline now performs a 10.8-second deterministic nucleus, compression, ignition, volumetric ejection, curved capture and settlement. Every particle uses a stable precomputed identity seed and immutable geographic home. No second renderer, network simulation or physics engine was introduced. Reduced motion skips the opening; replay and skip are supported.

12 cool orbital lights and 20 pale-aqua aircraft trajectories have distinct radii and short fading trails. Six optional sea routes are available but off by default. These are procedural trajectories with documented geographic anchors, not real tracked vehicles. Existing NOAA land/seafloor relief and interior matter remain intact; ocean particles have subtle motion.

Singapore retains the full city/street path and earlier fictional stories. New York uses one bundled lower-Manhattan OSM snapshot: 19,030 source segments and 24,236 stars. Street traffic follows road segments; softer activity wanders around sampled activity areas. No literal tracked people are represented. There is no claim of equal deep city coverage worldwide.

World commands and three guaranteed targets are implemented, serialized, and used by the manual navigation controls. [YC-WORLD-COMMANDS.md](YC-WORLD-COMMANDS.md) is the integration contract. Actual GPT-Live-1 code is still owned by YC and is not included in this milestone. His visible remote feature branch contained setup only when inspected at approximately 12:35 SGT.
