# Special destinations design note

This is an isolated, unpublished feasibility spike. Its decisions apply to this spike and do not revise the global `DESIGN.md`.

The spike retains Terra Astra's dark spatial stage, dominant geographic light, restrained interface, Georgia/Arial typography and relationship between cool geography and warm human activity. Both destinations use the existing point and line material factories, camera choreography, rendering loop, activity controls and resource ownership.

Palm Jumeirah adds source-derived trunk, fronds, crescent, coastal and mainland geometry. Warm urban activity follows the existing road sampler, now fed a compact set of named Palm and adjacent coastal roads. Mainland light is lifted, while trunk/frond filaments carry the focal warmth. Sparse turquoise points beyond the crescent suggest water activity; they do not represent boats, AIS observations or recorded tracks. Destination bounds and an interior fade soften the finite geographic extent.

Makkah adds planar mosque and urban geometry, a golden Kaaba anchor built from its mapped footprint and source-tagged height, and warm collective-flow particles. Independently scattered, overlapping radii replace the original twelve discrete bands. Gentle differences in circulation speed and density retain collective movement without a regular orbital or spiral lattice. Displayed density decreases with distance. The flow is interpretive: particles are neither individual people nor measured crowd density.

Destination configuration and a small asset adapter keep these additions bounded. Existing command payloads remain compatible; no renderer, shader or camera algorithm is replaced. Source geometry is traceable but not independently surveyed.

The finish disposition is **ship the feasibility handback**. Palm is **MERGE NOW, subject to owner approval**; its mainland light remains faint. Makkah is **PROMISING BUT POST-HACKATHON**: its street framing now retains more mosque context and its motion disclosure persists on mobile and tablet. Architectural recognition remains abstract, and the recorded motion deserves owner review. The recommendation does not authorize integration or publication.
